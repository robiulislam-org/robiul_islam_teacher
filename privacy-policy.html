Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide our services. This includes your name, contact information (WhatsApp number/Phone), and any details shared during the enrollment process.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for:

  • Scheduling and conducting English speaking sessions.
  • Managing payments and issuing receipts.
  • Communicating course updates or changes.

3. Data Protection

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your privacy is paramount, and all interactions during classes are kept confidential.

4. Online Sessions

Sessions are conducted via online platforms. While we ensure a safe learning environment, students are advised not to share sensitive personal information during group or individual sessions.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your privacy, please reach out to us at robiulislam.org or via WhatsApp.
