Payment & Refund Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Payment Terms

  • Advance Payment: All students are required to pay the monthly tuition fee in advance before starting the classes.
  • Monthly Renewal: For continuing students, the payment for the upcoming month must be completed by the 3rd day of that month.
  • Payment Methods: We accept payments via bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and direct Bank Transfer.

2. Refund Policy

We believe in transparency and fairness. Our refund rules are as follows:

  • Early Exit (Within 15 Days): If a student decides to discontinue the course within the first 15 days of the month, they will be charged only for the days they attended. The remaining balance will be refunded.
  • Late Exit (After 15 Days): If a student discontinues after the 15th day of the month, no refund will be issued for that specific month.
  • Refund Processing Time: Please note that it may take up to one (1) month to process and complete the refund transaction.

3. Instructor Commitment

  • Missed Classes: If the instructor (Robiul Islam) misses a scheduled class and is unable to provide a makeup session, the student is entitled to a full refund for that specific missed class.

4. Course Discontinuation

Students have the flexibility to leave the course at any time. However, the refund rules mentioned above will apply based on the date of discontinuation.

Contact for Refunds: To request a refund or for any payment-related queries, please contact us directly on WhatsApp at +8801733017521.
