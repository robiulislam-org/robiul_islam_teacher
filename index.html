2. How We Use Your Information

  • To contact you via WhatsApp and respond to your inquiry

6. Children's Privacy

Our Quran education services do accept young students. However, enrollment for minors must be initiated by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under age 13 without parental consent.

7. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Refund Policy

Transparent and fair refund terms for all our services.

1. General Refund Principle

We are committed to delivering high-quality education and digital marketing services. This policy outlines the conditions under which refunds may be granted.

2. Quran Education — Refund Terms

  • Free counseling session: No payment required. No refund applicable.
  • Full refund within 7 days: If unsatisfied after up to 2 classes, a full refund of the monthly fee may be requested within 7 days of the first class.
  • No refund after 7 days: After 7 days or more than 2 classes attended, no refund will be issued for that month.
  • Missed classes by student: No refund for classes missed without prior notice.

3. Digital Marketing Services — Refund Terms

  • Consultation: Free of charge. No refund applicable.
  • Before work begins: Full refund available if requested within 48 hours of payment before work starts.
  • After work begins: No refund for completed work once a project is underway.
  • Partial refund: If cancelled mid-project, a partial refund may be considered based on work completed — assessed case by case.
  • Ad spend: Any amounts spent on third-party platforms (Meta Ads, Google Ads, etc.) on your behalf are non-refundable.

4. How to Request a Refund

Contact us with your full name, phone number, service enrolled in, payment date, amount, and reason for the request. Refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days after approval.

5. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Failure to attend classes without prior notice
  • Dissatisfaction after the 7-day window has passed
  • Change of mind after the refund window has closed
  • Any third-party costs such as ad spend, domain, or hosting fees
